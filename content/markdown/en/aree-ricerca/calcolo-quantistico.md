## summary
Classical and quantum information theory, algorithms and implications for communication and cryptography.

## question
Which problems offer an advantage in the quantum model of computation?

## overview
The study of quantum computing encompasses algorithms, complexity and applications to cryptography, which the University of Milan’s 2026/2027 Quantum and Post-Quantum Computing materials approach through qubits, circuits and interference, leading to the Deutsch–Jozsa, Bernstein–Vazirani, Simon, Shor and Grover algorithms and quantum communication protocols.

## approach
Alongside teaching algorithms, I pursue an interest in classical and quantum information theory, exploring the role of entropy, the representation of states and the possibilities of communication. Optimisation and applications to machine learning fit within this perspective; the seven available modules support this exploration through mathematical foundations, circuits and quantum protocols.
