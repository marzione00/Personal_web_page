## introduction
The work on carbon nanostructures examines the relationship between geometry, topology and physical properties through computational methods and comparison with spectroscopic observations. In subsequent research, the focus shifted to traces generated by program execution, investigating ransomware detection and the interpretability of classifiers.

Variable selection, the assumptions embodied in models and the stability of explanations are methodological questions shared by these fields. Alongside published results, the work includes further study of quantum computing and the educational design of computer systems.
