## title
Physical foundations and simulation of quantum systems

## summary
From classical and statistical simulation to waves, interference, two-alternative states and composite systems. The module addresses the loss of interference, information and irreversibility, leading to the transition from quantum mechanics to computation.

## topics
- Classical and statistical simulation
- Waves, interference and measurement
- Qubits, composite systems and the environment
- Information and the construction of a quantum computer
