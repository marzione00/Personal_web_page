## title
Quantum algorithms: Bernstein–Vazirani and Simon

## summary
Two hidden-string reconstruction problems: the coefficients of a binary parity and the XOR relation between inputs with the same output. Oracles, interference and measurement are followed through to reconstruction of the answer.

## topics
- Binary parities and hidden strings
- Oracles and phase marking
- Bernstein–Vazirani
- Simon and binary constraints
