## title
Quantum and Post-Quantum Computing

## summary
The seven modules lead from physical foundations and mathematical tools to quantum circuits, algorithms and protocols, with slides available to read and download as PDFs and links in each module to related activities in Quantum Lab.

## university
University of Milan

## department
“Giovanni Degli Antoni” Department of Computer Science

## description
Starting from the simulation of physical systems and the mathematical tools used to describe them, the materials introduce quantum gates and circuits, on which the Deutsch–Jozsa, Bernstein–Vazirani, Simon, Shor and Grover algorithms are built; the course continues with BB84, superdense coding and error correction, before examining the operational limits of quantum key distribution and its relationship with post-quantum cryptography.
