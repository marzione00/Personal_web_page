## title
Quantum and Post-Quantum Computing

## summary
Seven modules on physical foundations, mathematical tools, circuits, algorithms and quantum protocols. The slides can be read and downloaded as PDFs.

## university
University of Milan

## department
“Giovanni Degli Antoni” Department of Computer Science

## description
After classical and statistical simulation and an introduction to the mathematical tools, the materials address quantum gates and circuits. They then cover the Deutsch–Jozsa, Bernstein–Vazirani, Simon, Shor and Grover algorithms, followed by BB84, superdense coding and error correction; a further discussion considers the operational limitations of quantum key distribution and its relationship with post-quantum cryptography.
