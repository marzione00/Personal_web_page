## titolo
Helicoid

## tema
Calculus and differential geometry

## formula
r(u,v) = (u cos(v), u sin(v), 0.23v)

## spiegazione
A straight line rotates while advancing along an axis, generating a ruled surface. The relationship between rotation and translation reveals the continuity of the form from different viewpoints.

## limiti
This is a finite portion of a parametric helicoid. Rotating the viewer moves the camera; it does not change the pitch of the surface.
