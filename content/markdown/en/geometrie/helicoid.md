# Helicoid

<!-- Edit the text below each ## heading; retain field names. -->

## titolo
Helicoid

## tema
Calculus and differential geometry

## formula
r(u,v) = (u cos(v), u sin(v), 0.23v)

## spiegazione
A straight line rotates while advancing along an axis, generating a ruled surface. The relationship between rotation and translation reveals the continuity of the form from different viewpoints.

## limiti
This is a finite portion of a parametric helicoid. Rotating the viewer moves the camera; it does not change the pitch of the surface.

## fonti
[SageMath — Helicoid](https://doc.sagemath.org/html/en/reference/riemannian_geometry/sage/geometry/riemannian_manifolds/surface3d_generators.html)

## osservazione
Follow the surface’s edge as you rotate it, observing how movement along the axis accompanies a change in orientation, then compare this continuity with the straight segments from which the shape is constructed.

## lettura
The first two coordinates are u cosine v and u sine v, while height is zero point two three v, coupling rotation with vertical translation.
