## titolo
Wave superposition

Quantum computing

## formula
f(x,y) = 0.34[cos(3x) + cos(3y)]
|ψ₁ + ψ₂|² = |ψ₁|² + |ψ₂|² + 2 Re(ψ₁*ψ₂)

## spiegazione
Peaks and troughs result from the sum of two periodic contributions. The figure introduces superposition visually, while the second relation shows the cross term responsible for interference between complex amplitudes.

## limiti
The surface is a snapshot of a synthetic real-valued field. It is not a probability density and is not normalised as a wave function. The viewer rotates the scene without simulating time evolution.

## osservazione
Compare crests and troughs using the surface grid and follow the two central sections, which reveal the superposition of periodic contributions as you change viewpoint on this single field configuration.

## lettura
The surface sums cosine of three x and cosine of three y, multiplied by zero point three four; the squared magnitude of two summed amplitudes includes their individual squared magnitudes and twice the real part of the first conjugated amplitude times the second.
