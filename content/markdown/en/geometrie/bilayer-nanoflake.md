# Bilayer graphene nanoflakes

## titolo
Bilayer graphene nanoflakes

## tema
Stacking and optical properties

## formula
R₂ = R₁ + (Δx, 0, h)
h = 2.36 dCC; Δx = 1 dCC or 0
Two separate graphs, with no interlayer covalent bonds.

## spiegazione
Two overlapping graphene fragments reveal how a relative shift changes ring alignment, introducing the role of interlayer interactions in the study of optical properties.

## limiti
Two ideal 40-carbon fragments have a fixed separation and an illustrative relative shift. Hydrogen, functional groups and orbitals are omitted. Colours distinguish the layers. Aligned and shifted views preserve each fragment’s geometry. The linked paper derives optical results from calculations on specific systems; this figure depicts arrangement and overlap without simulating absorption or charge transfer.

## fonti
[De Corato et al. — Optical Properties of Bilayer Graphene Nanoflakes](https://doi.org/10.1021/jp504222m)
[The bilayer-nanoflake publication](ricerca.html#pub-07)

## osservazione
Rotate the model to separate the two planes visually, then switch between shifted and aligned layers. Rings remain identical within each fragment while the relative positions of facing atoms change.

## lettura
The second fragment is translated horizontally and separated vertically from the first. All drawn bonds belong to individual layers.
