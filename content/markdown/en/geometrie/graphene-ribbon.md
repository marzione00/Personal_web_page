## titolo
Graphene nanoribbon

## tema
Carbon nanostructures

## formula
rₙₘ,α = na₁ + ma₂ + bα
a₁ = (3/2,√3/2),  a₂ = (3/2,−√3/2)
bA = (0,0),  bB = (1,0)

## spiegazione
A honeycomb network becomes a ribbon when its width is limited. Nodes and connections reveal the lattice, its edges and the difference between an extended sheet and a finite nanostructure.

## limiti
The coordinates describe an ideal lattice in nearest-neighbour distance units; the model does not come from a relaxed structure or a particular paper. Passivating hydrogen atoms are not shown.

## fonti
[Publications on nanostructures](ricerca.html#bibliografia)

## osservazione
Follow a row of hexagonal cells towards the ribbon’s edge, observing where the finite structure interrupts the network and how rotation helps distinguish the arrangement of nodes from overlaps in their projection onto the screen.

## lettura
Each position is the sum of integer multiples of two basis vectors and the offset of sublattice A or B; vector components are given in the formula.
