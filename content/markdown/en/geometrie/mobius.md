## titolo
Möbius strip

## tema
Topology and form

## formula
r(u,v) = ((1+0.35v cos(u/2))cos(u),
          (1+0.35v cos(u/2))sin(u), 0.35v sin(u/2))
0 ≤ u ≤ 2π,  −1 ≤ v ≤ 1

## spiegazione
A half-twist before joining the ends changes the relationship between boundary and surface. Following the strip explores a form in which a local distinction between two sides cannot be extended to a global choice.

## limiti
The formula parametrises a Möbius strip. The connection with bodily movement is personal and geometric; it does not attribute topological properties or therapeutic effects to yoga practice.

## osservazione
Follow the amber boundary until you return to the starting point, observing how one continuous path visits both apparent margins while the grid reveals the half-twist of the surface.

## lettura
Parameter u makes a full turn while v ranges from minus one to one; the width, scaled by zero point three five, makes a half turn around the central circle.
