# P688 schwarzite

## titolo
P688 schwarzite

## tema
Carbon and negative curvature

## formula
cos(2πx) + cos(2πy) + cos(2πz) = 0
K = k₁ · k₂ < 0 in the displayed neck.
P688: cubic Im-3m cell, a = 7.828 Å, 48 atoms per cell.

## limiti
The carbon positions follow the P688 crystallographic site (0.31952, 0.31952, 0.09373) and Im-3m symmetries; bonds join nearest neighbours at 1.452–1.467 Å. The initial view contains eight cells arranged 2×2×2, with 384 atoms, 64 complete hexagons and 48 complete octagons. The overview extends the periodic structure to 27 cells arranged 3×3×3, with 1,296 atoms. The finite boundaries cut rings and bonds that would continue through the periodic lattice. Dark ring faces distinguish front and rear bonds; they do not represent physical walls. The continuous surface appears in “Two curvatures” as a nodal guide for the Schwarz P family; its mean curvature can differ from zero, unlike the exact minimal surface. The carbon lattice and the reference surface are distinct descriptions. Colours, lighting and small offsets of the guide curves support legibility; carbon coordinates preserve the published model's distances, apart from uniform display scaling. This is a theoretical structure, with no new energy minimisation.

Rotate the lattice to follow repeating cavities in all three spatial directions, with hexagons outlining their curved walls and necks connecting adjacent cells. “Two curvatures” shows a blue axial section and an amber transverse section; at the pale point, they follow principal directions and bend in opposite senses. “P688 structure” broadens the view to the network of connected cavities.

## lettura
The sum of the cosines of two pi times each coordinate is zero. Gaussian curvature K is the product of the principal curvatures k one and k two. Their opposite signs make the product negative in the neck.
