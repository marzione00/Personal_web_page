## titolo
Torus

## tema
Geometry and periodicity

## formula
r(u,v) = ((R+r cos(v))cos(u),
          (R+r cos(v))sin(u), r sin(v))
R = 1,  r = 0.38

## spiegazione
Two periodic coordinates traverse the surface in distinct directions. The central hole and the continuity of paths make it a useful form for considering periodicity and topology.

## limiti
The torus is a parametric geometric object here. It does not automatically represent a state space or any particular material structure.

## fonti

## osservazione
Follow the amber cycle around the ring and compare it with the pale section around the tube, using the grid to distinguish the two periodic directions even when part of the surface is hidden behind the central opening.

## lettura
A circle of radius zero point three eight revolves around the central axis at distance one; u locates a point around the ring and v around the tube section.
