## titolo
Torus

## tema
Geometry and periodicity

## formula
r(u,v) = ((R+r cos(v))cos(u),
          (R+r cos(v))sin(u), r sin(v))
R = 1,  r = 0.38

## spiegazione
Two periodic coordinates traverse the surface in distinct directions. The central hole and the continuity of paths make it a useful form for considering periodicity and topology.

## limiti
The torus is a parametric geometric object here. It does not automatically represent a state space or any particular material structure.
