# Sierpiński tetrahedron

## titolo
Sierpiński tetrahedron

## tema
Fractals

## formula
S = ⋃ᵢ₌₁⁴ fᵢ(S),  fᵢ(x) = (x + vᵢ)/2
Self-similar dimension: log(4)/log(2) = 2

## spiegazione
Four scaled-down copies occupy a tetrahedron’s vertices. Repeating the construction produces a structure in which empty space is as recognisably organised as the solids.

## limiti
The scene shows three recursion levels, hence 64 tetrahedra. The stated dimension concerns the mathematical limit, not the finite rendered object.

## osservazione
Identify the largest tetrahedra you can recognise and zoom in to follow their subdivision, observing how voids and solids repeat a similar arrangement through the three recursion levels present in the scene.

## lettura
The set is the union of four copies obtained by halving the sum of a point and each vertex; the self-similar dimension is logarithm of four divided by logarithm of two, equal to two.
