## titolo
Negatively curved connections

## tema
Nanostructure geometry

## formula
r(u,v) = (0.64 cosh(v)cos(u), 0.64 cosh(v)sin(u), v)
Three families of necks, oriented along different axes.

## spiegazione
Longitudinal curves and circular sections describe three families of necks. The model highlights transitions between regions and the role of curvature in spatial connections.

## limiti
This geometric construction consists of hyperbolic necks scaled along the axes. It is not an atomistic model of a schwarzite: it encodes neither carbon coordination, stability nor bond lengths. The source documents the scientific relationship between topology and negatively curved carbon.

## fonti

## osservazione
Compare the three curve families, which distinguish necks along different axes, and, starting at the amber throat rings, follow the longitudinal curves towards the wider sections, rotating the figure to separate overlapping projections.

## lettura
The first two coordinates describe a circle with radius zero point six four times the hyperbolic cosine of v, while the third coordinate is v; necks are oriented along three directions.
