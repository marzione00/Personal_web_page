## titolo
Schwarz D surface

## tema
Physics and topology

## formula
cos(x)cos(y)cos(z) − sin(x)sin(y)sin(z) = 0

## spiegazione
The spatial network has connections and openings distinct from those of Schwarz P. Rotating it helps trace the passages and distinguish the regions separated by the surface.

## limiti
This periodic equation is a nodal representation of the D family, with a suitable choice of origin. It is not a relaxed atomic structure. Tiny isolated fragments caused by cutting the domain boundary are excluded.

## fonti
[Schwarzites and periodic surfaces — original research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6126744/)

## osservazione
Follow an opening as you rotate the surface, observing which passages become visible and which are hidden, then compare this organisation with the Schwarz P entry to recognise differences between the two spatial patterns.

## lettura
The product of the cosines of x, y and z minus the product of their sines equals zero.
