## titolo
Menger sponge

## tema
Fractals

## formula
M = ⋃ᵢ₌₁²⁰ fᵢ(M),  scale ratio = 1/3
Self-similar dimension: log(20)/log(3)

## spiegazione
Dividing a cube into 27 parts and removing seven generates a structure of interconnected cavities. Recursion reproduces this arrangement at progressively smaller scales.

## limiti
Two levels are displayed, corresponding to 400 cubes. Properties of the infinite limit should not be attributed indiscriminately to this finite model.

## fonti
[Wolfram — MengerMesh](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/MengerMesh.html)

## osservazione
Look through the larger openings and follow the edges of smaller cavities as rotation reveals internal passages; shared faces between adjacent cubes are omitted from the rendering to clarify the same 400-cell structure.

## lettura
The set is the union of twenty copies scaled to one third; its self-similar dimension is logarithm of twenty divided by logarithm of three.
