## titolo
Menger sponge

## tema
Fractals

## formula
M = ⋃ᵢ₌₁²⁰ fᵢ(M),  scale ratio = 1/3
Self-similar dimension: log(20)/log(3)

## spiegazione
Dividing a cube into 27 parts and removing seven generates a structure of interconnected cavities. Recursion reproduces this arrangement at progressively smaller scales.

## limiti
Two levels are displayed, corresponding to 400 cubes. Properties of the infinite limit should not be attributed indiscriminately to this finite model.
