# Schwarz P surface

## titolo
Schwarz P surface

## tema
Physics and topology

## formula
cos(x) + cos(y) + cos(z) = 0

## spiegazione
The surface separates space into two regions connected through periodic openings. Its symmetry and curvature offer a useful reference for understanding three-dimensional networks and porous structures.

## limiti
This is a nodal representation of Schwarz P on a finite domain, not an exact numerical solution of the zero-mean-curvature condition. The surface should not be confused with an atomistic schwarzite.

## fonti
[Schwarzites and periodic surfaces — original research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6126744/)

## osservazione
Compare the openings along the three directions with the illuminated sections, following how the surface joins neighbouring necks as the moving light distinguishes the near side from the interior.

## lettura
The sum of the cosines of coordinates x, y and z equals zero.
