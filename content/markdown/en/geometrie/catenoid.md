## titolo
Catenoid

## tema
Differential geometry

## formula
r(u,v) = (0.8 cosh(v)cos(u),
          0.8 cosh(v)sin(u), 0.8v)

## spiegazione
Rotating a catenary generates a surface with a central neck. The contrast between the two principal curvatures makes visible a geometry encountered in the study of minimal surfaces.

## limiti
This is a parametric portion of a catenoid, discretised for display. The construction does not simulate the stability of a soap film or a physical experiment.
