## titolo
Catenoid

## tema
Differential geometry

## formula
r(u,v) = (0.8 cosh(v)cos(u),
          0.8 cosh(v)sin(u), 0.8v)

## spiegazione
Rotating a catenary generates a surface with a central neck. The contrast between the two principal curvatures makes visible a geometry encountered in the study of minimal surfaces.

## limiti
This is a parametric portion of a catenoid, discretised for display. The construction does not simulate the stability of a soap film or a physical experiment.

## fonti
[Reproducible parametrisation included in the project](geometrie.html#catenoid)

## osservazione
Rotate the surface to view it from the side, follow its narrowing towards the central neck and observe how both ends widen, comparing the profile with the lines that travel around the figure.

## lettura
At height zero point eight v, the circular section has radius zero point eight times the hyperbolic cosine of v; angle u runs around the circle.
