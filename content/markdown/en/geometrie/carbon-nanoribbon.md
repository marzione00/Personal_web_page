# Graphene nanoribbons

## titolo
Graphene nanoribbons

## tema
Atomic edges and vibrational signatures

## formula
Honeycomb lattice: two atoms per primitive cell.
Nearest neighbours: dᵢⱼ = 1 in model units.
Armchair and zigzag: two orientations of the same lattice.

## spiegazione
Cutting a graphene sheet leaves an atomic edge whose properties can be investigated through spectroscopy and calculations. The two views make armchair and zigzag terminations recognisable.

## limiti
The finite fragments contain 80 and 84 carbon atoms respectively. Coordinates describe an ideal planar lattice; hydrogen and other terminating groups are omitted. The fragments have different finite dimensions and illustrate edge patterns. The linked Raman and HREELS papers examine specific structures through measurements and calculations. These figures introduce their geometry; peak assignments and frequencies belong to the papers’ experimental and theoretical results.

## osservazione
Observe the atom pairs along the armchair edge, then select the zigzag view to compare its different boundary pattern. Follow the hexagons inward and examine the ends of the finite fragment.

## lettura
The planar lattice has two atoms per primitive cell and unit bond lengths in this model. The cut direction determines an armchair or zigzag termination.
