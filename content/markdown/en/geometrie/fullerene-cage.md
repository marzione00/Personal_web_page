# C₆₀ fullerene

## titolo
C₆₀ fullerene

## tema
Carbon cages and positive curvature

## formula
V = 60, E = 90, F = 32
12 pentagons + 20 hexagons
V − E + F = 2; three neighbours per vertex.

## spiegazione
Twelve pentagons allow a hexagonal carbon network to close into a cage, revealing how ring arrangement accompanies the curvature of a closed carbon surface.

## limiti
This is an ideal truncated icosahedron with equal bond lengths; molecular C₆₀ has different bond lengths. Dark faces clarify depth and pentagons have a warmer tint. The cage introduces fullerenes. The linked work on two C₂₈ clathrates concerns distinct crystalline structures, whose reconstruction requires specific coordinates.

## osservazione
Follow a pentagon and compare its boundary with neighbouring hexagons. Rotate the cage to trace the continuous network and the three bonds meeting at each atom. Compare this convex cage with the periodic saddle openings of the schwarzite.

## lettura
Sixty vertices and ninety edges enclose thirty-two faces: twelve pentagons and twenty hexagons. Vertices minus edges plus faces equals two.
