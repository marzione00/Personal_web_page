## titolo
Lattice and basis

## tema
Post-quantum computing

## formula
Λ(B) = {n₁b₁ + n₂b₂ + n₃b₃ : n₁,n₂,n₃ ∈ ℤ}

## spiegazione
Integer combinations of three vectors generate the lattice points. Highlighted segments distinguish the basis from the point network, providing an initial visual reference for lattice problems.

## limiti
The model has three dimensions and finitely many points. It does not represent the dimensions, parameters or full structure of ML-KEM, nor every approach to post-quantum cryptography. The source concerns the cryptographic context, not this illustrative lattice.

## fonti
[NIST — ML-KEM](https://www.nist.gov/publications/module-lattice-based-key-encapsulation-mechanism-standard)

## osservazione
Identify the three highlighted vectors and follow the points arranged along their directions, rotating the figure to see how integer combinations of the same vectors describe the lattice even when the projection makes some elements overlap.

## lettura
The lattice is the set of all combinations of the three basis vectors with integer coefficients.
