## titolo
Lattice and basis

## tema
Post-quantum computing

## formula
Λ(B) = {n₁b₁ + n₂b₂ + n₃b₃ : n₁,n₂,n₃ ∈ ℤ}

## spiegazione
Integer combinations of three vectors generate the lattice points. Highlighted segments distinguish the basis from the point network, providing an initial visual reference for lattice problems.

## limiti
The model has three dimensions and finitely many points. It does not represent the dimensions, parameters or full structure of ML-KEM, nor every approach to post-quantum cryptography. The source concerns the cryptographic context, not this illustrative lattice.
