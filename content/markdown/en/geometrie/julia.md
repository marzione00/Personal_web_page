## titolo
Julia set

## tema
Fractals and dynamical systems

## formula
zₙ₊₁ = zₙ² + c
c = −0.74543 + 0.11301i

## spiegazione
Iterating the same rule produces intricate boundaries between escaping points and points that remain bounded. Colour depends on escape time and reveals details at different scales.

## limiti
The fractal is planar. The viewer allows its plane to be oriented in space without inventing a three-dimensional volume. The image uses 180 iterations: it is a finite approximation, not a representation of every mathematical detail.

## osservazione
Zoom in to observe colour changes along the boundaries and compare nearby regions with different escape times, remembering that zoom enlarges a representation computed with a finite number of iterations and the available resolution.

## lettura
At each iteration, square complex number z and add c, equal to minus zero point seven four five four three plus zero point one one three zero one times the imaginary unit.
