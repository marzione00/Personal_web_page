## spiegazione
Iterating the same rule produces intricate boundaries between escaping points and points that remain bounded. Colour depends on escape time and reveals details at different scales.

## limiti
The fractal is planar. The viewer allows its plane to be oriented in space without inventing a three-dimensional volume. The image uses 180 iterations: it is a finite approximation, not a representation of every mathematical detail.
