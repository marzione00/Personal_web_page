# Gyroid

## titolo
Gyroid

## tema
Physics and geometry

## formula
sin(x)cos(y) + sin(y)cos(z) + sin(z)cos(x) = 0

## spiegazione
Passages and openings connect the surface in all three spatial directions. This form evokes the study of periodic structures and materials whose internal geometry influences their properties.

## limiti
The formula generates a nodal approximation to the minimal surface, not the exact gyroid. The model shows a bounded portion of the domain and contains no atoms or chemical bonds.

## fonti
[Schwarzites and periodic surfaces — original research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6126744/)

## osservazione
Rotate the surface and follow the openings as they become visible from different viewpoints, revealing how passages develop in three spatial directions and how the represented portion extends towards the domain’s boundaries.

## lettura
The sum of sine x times cosine y, sine y times cosine z, and sine z times cosine x equals zero.
