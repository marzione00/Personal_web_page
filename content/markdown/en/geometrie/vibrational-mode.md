## titolo
Out-of-plane deformation

## tema
Carbon nanostructures

## formula
uᶻ(x,y) = 1.1 sin(0.7x) cos(0.42y)

## spiegazione
The ideal lattice is displaced out of its plane according to a regular field. One can observe how deformation changes the ribbon’s shape while leaving lattice connectivity recognisable.

## limiti
The field is synthetic. It is not a normal mode calculated from a dynamical matrix, does not reproduce a Raman frequency and is not an ab initio result by the author. The figure deliberately distinguishes geometric deformation from physical vibrational analysis.
