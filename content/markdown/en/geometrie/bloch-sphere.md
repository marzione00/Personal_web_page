# Bloch sphere

## titolo
Bloch sphere

## tema
Quantum computing

## formula
|ψ⟩ = cos(θ/2)|0⟩ + exp(iφ) sin(θ/2)|1⟩
r = (sinθ cosφ, sinθ sinφ, cosθ)

## spiegazione
A point on the sphere represents a pure state of a qubit, up to a global phase. The highlighted vector selects a state; meridians and parallels help read the two angular coordinates.

## limiti
Rotating the viewer changes the viewpoint; it does not apply a gate to the qubit. Mixed states occupy the interior of the sphere. This representation does not describe an arbitrary multi-qubit state.

## osservazione
Follow the amber arrow to the represented pure state and compare it with the angular arcs, meridians and parallels, distinguishing a change of viewpoint from a change in the quantum state.

## lettura
The state is cosine of half theta times state zero plus the exponential of i phi times sine of half theta times state one; the vector coordinates are sine theta cosine phi, sine theta sine phi, and cosine theta.

## legenda

blue | sphere meridians
mint | sphere parallels
pale | reference axes
