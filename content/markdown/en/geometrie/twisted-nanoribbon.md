# Deformed nanoribbon

## titolo
Deformed nanoribbon

## tema
Lattice shape and electronic structure

## formula
R(x,y) = (x, y cos θ(x), y sin θ(x))
θ(x) = 0.16 x
The planar ribbon’s connectivity is preserved.

## spiegazione
Twisting moves the hexagons out of plane and changes the orientation of ribbon regions, providing a visual introduction to the relationship between structural distortion and electronic properties.

## limiti
This illustrative geometric deformation acts on 80 atoms. It preserves connectivity while changing some distances and bond angles. Its twist parameter is chosen for visual clarity. The 2018 paper examines specific ribbons and control of structural distortion; this figure introduces the question without claiming an optimised structure, a calculated bandgap or a normal vibrational mode.

## fonti

## osservazione
Follow an edge from one end to the other as it turns around the long axis. Compare central rings with those at the ends and rotate the model to distinguish the imposed twist from perspective.

## lettura
Each transverse coordinate rotates through an angle that increases linearly along the ribbon axis. The bond list remains identical to the planar ribbon.
