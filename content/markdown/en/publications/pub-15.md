## title
Topological invariants of Möbius-like graphenic nanostructures

## authors
M. V. Putz, M. De Corato, G. Benedek, J. Sedlar, A. Graovac, O. Ori

## where
Topological Modelling of Nanostructures and Extended Systems · Springer, pp. 229–244

## plainTitle
The topology of carbon nanoribbons with a Möbius twist

## plainSummary
Closing a nanoribbon into a Möbius form changes its structural connections, which the work describes through topological indices that quantify differences introduced by the twist and allow their organisation to be compared.

## explanation
The chapter compares carbon-nanoribbon models with ordinary closure and with Möbius-type closure. It calculates topological invariants and studies their behaviour as the system grows, examining compactness and connections between distant parts of the network. These quantities are discussed in relation to the stability of the structures considered. The work concerns mathematical analysis of models in which twisting changes the lattice topology.
