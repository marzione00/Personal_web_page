## title
The topological background of schwarzite physics

## authors
G. Benedek, M. Bernasconi, E. Cinquanta, L. D’Alessio, M. De Corato

## where
The Mathematics and Topology of Fullerenes · Springer, pp. 217–247

## plainTitle
Schwarzite geometry and the properties of porous carbon

## plainSummary
The chapter surveys the connection between negatively curved carbon networks, growth conditions and physical properties.

## explanation
The review considers disordered, sponge-like schwarzites and periodic structures, relating topology to stability and possible formation pathways. It also discusses electronic properties, electron–vibration interactions and atomic-dynamics simulations that follow the network’s evolution. The contribution provides an overview of the physics of these structures in the context of the studies available at the time of publication.
