## title
Raman fingerprints of atomically precise graphene nanoribbons

## authors
I. A. Verzhbitskiy, M. De Corato, A. Ruini, et al.

## where
Nano Letters 16(6), 3442–3447

## plainTitle
The Raman signature of graphene nanoribbons

## plainSummary
Light scattered by a material can reveal information about nanoribbon width, edges and the chemical groups attached to them.

## explanation
Raman spectroscopy is compared with ab initio calculations to understand which nanoribbon features correspond to the measured signals. The work examines the effects of width, edge geometry and chemical functionalisation. Certain low-frequency vibrations are particularly sensitive to the edges; the dependence of one spectral band on the excitation is also studied. These correspondences help identify nanoribbon structure through spectroscopic measurement.
