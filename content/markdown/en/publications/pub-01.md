## title
L’Intelligenza Artificiale negli enti locali

## authors
D. Bruschi, A. Ferrara, M. De Corato and S. Castano (eds.)

## where

## plainTitle
Artificial intelligence and local-government services

## plainSummary
Through a survey of 72 municipalities and a prototype for classifying municipal website pages, the volume examines uses of artificial intelligence in local government in relation to the skills and responsibilities accompanying its adoption.

## explanation
The volume reports a project carried out between June 2024 and March 2025, combining training, an exploratory survey with 72 participating municipalities and a prototype for automatically classifying municipal website pages. The responses reveal interest in automating repetitive tasks, data analysis and decision support, together with a demand for transparent models and human oversight. The findings concern the administrations that took part in the survey and offer a basis for designing pilot initiatives accompanied by training.
