## title
Topological versus physical and chemical properties of negatively curved carbon surfaces

## authors
M. De Corato, M. Bernasconi, L. D’Alessio, O. Ori, M. V. Putz, G. Benedek

## where
Topological Modelling of Nanostructures and Extended Systems · Springer, pp. 105–136

## plainTitle
Topological models and properties of carbon surfaces

## plainSummary
By representing atoms as nodes and bonds as connections, the work relates some physical properties of carbon surfaces to simpler topological descriptions through which differently organised structures can be compared.

## explanation
The chapter studies negatively curved carbon surfaces, or schwarzites, comparing topological arguments with ab initio calculations. It examines the approximations under which connectivity helps describe vibrational spectra and how defects change those spectra. For certain planar schwarzites, it also compares calculated stability with indications from the Wiener index. The relationship between a network model and a physical description is discussed alongside the assumptions that delimit its validity.
