## title
Interpretability of machine learning algorithms: How these techniques can correctly guess the physical laws?

## authors
M. De Corato, A. Ferrara, S. Salini

## where
Statistics and Data Science Conference · EGEA

## plainTitle
Algorithm interpretability on data derived from physical laws

## plainSummary
The work compares what a model appears to learn with known laws to examine the relationship between prediction and explanation.

## explanation
The authors use astronomical data and data constructed from physical laws, also adding noisy variables. They train several algorithms, including neural networks, graphical models, Random Forests and statistical classifiers, and examine their behaviour through interpretation techniques. Comparison with the law that generated the data allows the learned dependencies to be assessed against the phenomenon. The contribution thus offers a way to evaluate interpretability before addressing situations in which physical relationships are less well understood.
