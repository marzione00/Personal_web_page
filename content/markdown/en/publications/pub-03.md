## title
Ransomware detection using sample entropy and graphical models: A methodology for explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) in cybersecurity

## authors
D. Bruschi, M. De Corato, A. Ferrara, S. Salini

## where
Applied Stochastic Models in Business and Industry 41(6), e70061

## plainTitle
Recognising ransomware from processor signals

## plainSummary
A program also leaves traces in processor activity: this study uses them to identify suspicious behaviour and make classification understandable.

## explanation
Hardware counters record aspects of processor activity during program execution. Sample entropy summarises their regularity over time; this information feeds probabilistic graphical models, which describe relationships between variables. Comparison with other classifiers allows the ability to distinguish ransomware to be evaluated, while the structure of the models helps examine the reasons for decisions. The result is an interpretable analytical methodology evaluated on the study’s data; operational deployment requires testing under the intended conditions of use.
